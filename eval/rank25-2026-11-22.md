# Review requests to teams (2026-11-22)

PRD `RANK-25` changes how review requests to teams are ranked, and asks for a
measurement before it ships (`RANK-7`, `RANK-24`). This is that measurement, for
the three ranking agents' models and basic ranking, with `prompts/v4.md` as
changed for teams. Runs: `eval/runs/*-rank25-*`.

## Nothing else got worse

The 36 hand-ranked items (`eval/ground_truth.yaml`, three trials) and the 20
production items (`eval/next_steps.yaml`, three trials, one for basic ranking).
"Before" is the measurement of 2026-11-18 with the product owner's labels
(`eval/next-step-2026-11-18.md`).

| Model | Kendall tau, before → now | Next step, 36 items | Next step, 20 items |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gemini-3.8-flash` (low) | 0.679 → 0.666 | 88% → 86% | 55% → 50% |
| `claude-sonnet-5-5` | 0.620 → 0.650 | 76% → 81% | 43% → 42% |
| `gpt-6.1-sol` (low) | 0.671 → 0.636 | 90% → 92% | 47% → 47% |
| `gemini-decision-1` (basic) | 0.517 → 0.540 | 81% → 83% | 40% → 45% |

The changes are within the spread of the trials, except OpenAI's tau. Six
trials averaged 0.636. A control run on the same day, with the prompt before
this change, gave 0.652 ± 0.019, so most of the drop is the model, not the
prompt.

## What a team's prompt does

The six PRs in the fixtures that ask `dart-lang/dart-ecosystem-team` for review,
with the team in Settings three ways: not sent (no team, or one with no prompt,
`eval/teams_none.yaml`), with "I have a small level of responsibility for this
team." (`teams_small`) and with "I review most of this team's PRs."
(`teams_most`). The mean score of the six, and their usual next step:

| Model | None | Small | Most |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gemini-3.8-flash` (low) | 0.05, Not yours | 0.31, Review | 0.38, Review |
| `claude-sonnet-5-5` | 0.08, Not yours | 0.18, Review or Not yours | 0.47, Review |
| `gpt-6.1-sol` (low) | 0.06, Not yours | 0.33, Review | 0.40, Review |
| `gemini-decision-1` (basic) | 0.21, Not yours | 0.50, Review | 0.54, Review |

- **No prompt:** all four keep the team's PRs as Not yours, low, which matches
  the product owner's labels for these items (83% to 100%).
- **With a prompt:** they become Review, ordered none < small < most for every
  model. Claude separates "small" from "most" clearly. The others barely do:
  OpenAI, Gemini and basic ranking rank a small share almost like most of it.
- **The rules hold.** `tools#2710` is stacked on an unreviewed draft, and every
  model makes it Elsewhere. A PR that also names a person (`tools#2715`,
  `tools#2899`) is put a little lower, as `RANK-25` says. Gemini makes
  `core#1007` Waiting, because its CLA check failed.
- **The next step "exact" numbers in the runs are low (22% to 83%) because the
  labels were wrong, not the models.** Claude wrote them from the rules, as Not
  yours for a PR that also names a person and Review for the stacked PR. The
  models' answers, Review a little lower and Elsewhere, are what `RANK-25`
  asks for.
